📖 Economy Guide

Everything you need to master EconomyBot. From your first command to running the richest server in Discord.

🚀 Getting Started
From zero to your first coins in under 5 minutes
1
Add EconomyBot to Your Server
Click the button below and authorize EconomyBot with the required permissions. It needs to send messages and use slash commands.
+ Add to Discord →
2
Open Your Economy Hub
Type the command below in any channel. Your personal hub opens instantly — no setup required. First-time users get a 500 coin welcome bonus!
/economy
3
Complete Your First Work Shift
Press the 💼 Work button in your hub. You'll be asked to choose a job and complete a quick interaction. Earn your first 100–500 coins!
/work → Choose job → Earn coins
4
Deposit to Your Bank
Keep your coins safe by depositing them to the bank. Wallet coins can be lost through crime or robbery. Bank coins earn 2.5% daily interest!
/bank → Deposit → Enter amount
5
Claim Your Daily Reward
Come back every day to claim your daily bonus. Build up a streak for multiplied rewards — up to 7x for a 7-day streak!
/daily → Claim → Build your streak 🔥

🏦 Banking & Investment
Make your money work for you
🏦
Bank Account

Deposit any amount. Earn 2.5% daily interest on your balance. Coins in the bank are protected from robbery and crime fines.

/bank deposit [amount]
📈
Stock Market

Invest in 5 virtual stocks that fluctuate based on server activity. Hold for dividends or trade for profit. High risk, high reward.

/invest [stock] [amount]
💡 Pro tip: Keep 80% of your coins in the bank at all times. The 2.5% daily interest on 100K coins = 2,500 free coins per day, forever. That's over 900K coins per year from interest alone.
