Frequently Asked Questions

Add EconomyBot to your server using the button at the top of this page, then type /economy in any channel. Your hub opens instantly — no setup required. You'll receive a 500 coin welcome bonus on your first use. Check the guide for a full getting-started walkthrough.
First check the status page to see if there's an outage. If all systems are green, make sure EconomyBot has permission to read and send messages in that channel. Try using the command in a different channel. If issues persist, report it in our Discord server.
Coins in your wallet can be lost through crime fails (20% fine), being robbed by other users, or gambling losses. Check your /profile transaction history to see recent activity. Coins in your bank are always safe. If you believe there's a bug, please use /bugreport with your username and approximate time of the loss.
You can cancel anytime from your Stripe customer portal — the link was sent to you via DM when you first subscribed. You can also use the /manage command in Discord to access your billing. Your perks continue until the end of your billing period. No refunds are issued for partial periods.
Yes! Your premium perks (bonus daily rewards, reduced cooldowns, inventory slots, profile customization) are tied to your Discord account, not any specific server. Your perks follow you everywhere EconomyBot is installed.
Yes! Use /config (requires Manage Server permission) to customize: the currency name and symbol, per-command cooldown multipliers, enable or disable specific commands, set a log channel for economy activity, and configure starting balance for new users.
Bank interest is paid out at 2.5% of your total bank balance every 24 hours. Interest compounds daily — so the more you have in the bank, the faster it grows. There's no cap on how much interest you can earn. Depositing is always free; withdrawals are also free.
Crime has a chance of failure based on your level. When caught, you receive a 20% wallet fine (coins in the bank are safe). The success rate improves as you level up. Elite premium users get a 10% bonus to crime success rate. If you want safer income, stick to /work and /search.
Use /reset (requires Administrator permission). You can reset a single user's balance or the entire server economy. This action requires double confirmation and is irreversible — there is no way to restore data after a reset, so proceed with caution.
